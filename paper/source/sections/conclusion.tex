\section{Conclusion}
We implement a deterministic artifact-completion gate within JiuwenSwarm and document a historical real, traceable research workflow with an independently reviewed ICLR manuscript. Its controlled artifact pilot identifies all 20 specified blocking faults but does not certify scientific truth. A separate paired reasoning pilot compares one external diagnostic retry against one generic retry on the same 24 synthetic items. Diagnostic accuracy is 12/24 versus 11/24 for generic retry, with discordance 1/0 and exact $p=1.0$: the evidence is inconclusive. Complete records, accepted alternative answers, unequal token costs, failed engineering runs, and operator amendments are disclosed. Broader reasoning enhancement remains an empirical question requiring larger, fresh evaluations.
