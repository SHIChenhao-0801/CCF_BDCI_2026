\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Inference-time reasoning and revision.}
Chain-of-thought prompting supplies intermediate reasoning examples as context \citep{jason20224a94a8d1}. Our solver instead returns a strict structured answer; we neither reproduce chain-of-thought experiments nor compare explanation styles. Self-Refine iteratively uses model-generated feedback and revision \citep{aman2023820a9974}. Here the diagnostic is supplied by executable constraints, only one retry is permitted, and no learned feedback model is introduced. Huang et al. examine limitations of intrinsic reasoning self-correction without reliable external feedback \citep{jie2023fd36c5c8}. Their setting motivates separating unassisted reconsideration from external assistance; it does not predict a positive effect on our synthetic items.

\paragraph{Tool-using and reflective agents.}
ReAct combines reasoning and actions in interactive tasks \citep{shunyu2022f4fcac2c}; Reflexion uses verbal feedback in an agent framework \citep{noah2023b7573199}. These works contextualize agents that use environmental evidence. Our comparison concerns a compact diagnostic at a fixed answer-repair opportunity, not an unrestricted action loop or repeated verbal memory. The research pipeline's Reflection module analyzes experiment results; it is separate from the solver's diagnostic retry and should not be counted as another answer-repair arm.

\paragraph{Evidence contracts.}
The Artifact Provenance Gate evaluates local files and runtime state. Answer validation and artifact validation serve different roles: a SAT checker can accept any satisfying assignment, whereas the gate can only determine whether required reporting evidence exists and is interpretable. Citation-key resolution likewise proves linkage in a compiled document, not semantic citation support. We present a practical framework extension and controlled evaluation, without claiming that artifact gates, external feedback, or paired testing are previously unknown concepts. Related-work context was curated from primary-source metadata and abstracts; this paper does not imply a systematic review of all full texts.
